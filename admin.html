A passcode is generated here and shown once
These names are the choices in every facilitator drop-down: the dashboards, potential initiatives and Summit HQ. Signing in comes from People & Access; the passcode here only matters for anyone still saving with a Hub passcode. A lost passcode is reset, not looked up. Removing someone takes effect on their next save.
